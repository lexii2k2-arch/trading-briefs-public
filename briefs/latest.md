Charts were not directly accessible, so levels are execution rules rather than exact price calls.

For all long candidates:

- Confirmation: reclaim/hold of VWAP, higher low after the first pullback, and break of the first 5- or 15-minute high.

- First target: premarket high or opening-drive high.

For short/fade candidates:

- Only consider downside on failed reclaim setups, not blind shorts into the opening flush.
- Confirmation: failed VWAP reclaim plus lower high and break of opening-range low.
- Invalidation: reclaim of VWAP and acceptance above the failed-breakdown level.
